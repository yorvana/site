FAQ

A few details before you apply.

Is Yorvana free?

Yorvana is planned as freemium: 1 vehicle free, with unlimited vehicles available through a one-time lifetime purchase after beta.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in a folder you pick. Nothing is uploaded by Yorvana.

Will there be iOS?

Not at launch. The architecture does not preclude it.

How do I leave the beta?

Uninstall Yorvana, or opt out through the Play closed-test page. Your local data stays where you put it.
